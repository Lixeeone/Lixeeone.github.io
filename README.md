## About

I am an undergraduate student at the **School of Information and Intelligent Science, Donghua University**, advised by **Lecturer Ge Zhang**. I am also a research intern at the **BCMI Laboratory, Shanghai Jiao Tong University**, advised by **Professor Wei-Long Zheng**.

My research centers on learning systems that combine mathematical structure, decentralized interaction, and multimodal reasoning. I currently co-lead **RBBench**, a project on rubric-based evaluation of agentic coding trajectories, and founded **LXTZL Lab** at Donghua University.

## Research Interests

- **Scientific Machine Learning:** graph neural networks and generative models for scientific simulation, prediction, and design.
- **Multi-Agent Learning:** decentralized representation learning, cooperative decision-making, game-theoretic learning, and coordination.
- **Multimodal and Efficient Learning:** multimodal representations, vision-language models, and resource-efficient deep learning.
